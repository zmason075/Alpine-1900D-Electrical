▶What this means

    DC system only. Load meters show DC load %, simplified: 20% on each side with both gens online, 40% on the one online gen otherwise. Not modeled: battery failure and automatic HED trips at 325A and 275A (gen ties: click a closed tie to trip it open, an open tie to close it manually so the gen bus runs on battery, or a tripped tie to reset it; ties also open on a center bus short, that gen's overvoltage, or when they have not sensed 28V). Where a bus or line has more than one source, its color alternates between those sources. Cockpit switches (bottom left of the diagram): click above or below a switch to move it. GEN TIES: MAN CLOSE closes both gen ties, OPEN holds them open. Ignition: ON or STARTER ONLY begins a start (the starter relay needs power); OFF after an ON start leaves the engine running. Volt select (left of the diagram): click the knob or a label to choose what the voltmeter reads: EXT PWR, CTR BUS, L GEN, R GEN, TPL BUS or BATT (the order on the cockpit knob).
